Advertising policy

Free checks are funded modestly and transparently. These rules bind us, not just advertisers.

  • Every ad slot is reserved in the layout (no content shift) and labelled Sponsored.
  • Currently, slots carry first-party house messages only (our own projects and direct sponsors). Third-party ad networks, if ever added, will load only with your consent, in sandboxed frames, under this same policy.
  • No deceptive creatives: no fake security warnings, no fake download or scan buttons, no impersonation of system dialogs, no malware — a security product that runs scareware ads has no business existing.
  • Ads never appear on the vault or other authenticated/private pages, and never adjacent to a security warning in a way that implies endorsement.
  • Density is capped: at most one slot above the fold and two in-flow per page.
  • We honour Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals for any future personalised advertising; non-personalised is the default.

Direct sponsors: security tools with a genuine product (password managers, backup, VPNs) may enquire via the repository contact channels. Revenue from advertising is reported in aggregate; user data is never part of any ad deal.
