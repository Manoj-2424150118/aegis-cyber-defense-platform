The service

Aegis provides security-checking tools on an as-is basis. Results are heuristic guidance based on the evidence described on each tool page; they are not a guarantee that content is safe or unsafe. You remain responsible for your decisions.

Acceptable use

  • Do not use the tools to attack, scan or probe systems you do not own or have permission to test, beyond the public-header and DNS information any browser receives.
  • Do not abuse the free tier (automated scraping, quota evasion) — use the API when it launches.
  • Do not upload material you have no right to process. Uploaded files are analysed in memory and discarded.

Accounts and the vault

You are responsible for your account password and your vault master password. The master password cannot be recovered by design; losing it means losing vault contents. We may suspend accounts that violate these terms.
