What it checks

  • Parcel/customs, digital-arrest and police-impersonation scripts
  • Family-emergency 'new number' messages, tech-support (AnyDesk/TeamViewer) scams
  • Fake loan, investment-group and task-earning playbooks, in English and Hinglish

  • Links and payment handles inside the message, scored with the link engine

How it works

Playbook detectors look for the combination of hook, pressure and ask that defines each scam family — not single alarming words. Every finding quotes the pattern it matched, and genuine safety warnings are sentence-filtered before bait is judged.

What the result means

  • Any message asking you to share an OTP, install a screen-sharing app, or pay to release a parcel is a scam. Full stop — no legitimate body does this.
  • 'Digital arrest' does not exist: Indian police and courts do not arrest over video call or demand money to clear a case.
  • For family emergencies, call the person's old number or a relative — never the new number that messaged you.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do if I already responded?

Stop contact, call your bank's official number to freeze payments, report at cybercrime.gov.in (India) or your national portal, and warn the person being impersonated.

Does it work for WhatsApp messages?

Yes — paste the text from any channel: SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram, email or social DMs.

Why did my bank's real warning score clean?

Because it should. The checker strips disclaimer sentences ('never share your OTP…') before judging bait — a warning about scams is not a scam.
