Refund policy

Paid plans are not on sale yet, so no one can currently be charged. This policy is published in advance and will govern Pro from its first day of sale.

  • Cooling-off: full refund within 7 days of first purchase, no questions asked, via the original payment method.
  • Renewals: cancel anytime in settings; access continues to the end of the paid period. Renewal charges are refundable within 48 hours if the plan was unused in the new period.
  • Our failures: if a paid feature is withdrawn or the service is down for more than 72 consecutive hours, affected periods are refunded pro-rata on request.
  • Taxes and invoices: GST/VAT-compliant invoices are issued for every charge; prices shown at checkout will always include applicable taxes.
  • Chargebacks: talk to us first — refunds above are faster than a dispute for both sides.
